Privacy policy for Sol

Sol is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own laptop.

  • Sol uses Google sign-in only to send commands to the owner's own Google Assistant, for example "turn on the lights". Sol does not use Google data for anything else.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's laptop. It is never shared, sold or sent to anyone else.
  • Sol does not collect data from other people. There are no other users.
  • The owner can remove Sol's access at any time in their Google account (Security, Third-party apps).

Last updated: 3 November 2026.
